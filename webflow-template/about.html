About Hanabi

A small room with a big fire

Hanabi means fireworks — the short, bright moment we try to create with every plate. Everything else is slow: curing fish, washing rice, tending coals.

01What we believe
01Season first

The menu follows the fish market, not the other way around. Some dishes last a week.

02Fire with patience

Binchotan is lit at dawn and settles for hours before the first skewer hits the grill.

03Nothing wasted

Bones become broth, trim becomes tartare, rice becomes crackers for the bar.

02Our journey

Kenji opens a tiny sushi bar in a harbor side street.

2021
The robata arrives

We move next door and build a charcoal grill into the room.

2023
Omakase nights

Fourteen courses, twelve seats, twice a week.

2026
Still cooking

Same rice recipe, a bigger team and a short list of sake we love.
