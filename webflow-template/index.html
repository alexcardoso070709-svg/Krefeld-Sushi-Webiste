01Our story

We slice fish the moment you order and cook over charcoal that burns for hours — nothing rushed, nothing wasted.

Hanabi started as a six-seat counter in a harbor side street. Today we are a little bigger, but the rules are the same: seasonal fish, rice seasoned every hour, and a grill that is lit before sunrise.

— Kenji Arata, head chef Read our story
