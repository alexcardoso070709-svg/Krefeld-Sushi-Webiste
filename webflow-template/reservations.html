Good to know

Questions & answers

Still unsure? Call us between 14:00 and 17:00 and we'll help you plan your visit.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes — the bar and four high tables are kept for walk-in guests every evening.

Can you cater for allergies?

Most dishes can be adjusted. For the omakase, tell us 48 hours in advance.

Is there a cancellation fee?

Tables are free to cancel. Omakase seats can be cancelled up to 24 hours before.

Do you host private events?

The whole room seats 48 guests. Send us a message via the contact page.
